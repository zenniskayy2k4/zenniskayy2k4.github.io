# Hey, I'm ZennisKayy 👋

Final-year Information Security student at **HCMC University of Science (HCMUS)**. I spend most of my time breaking things apart to understand how they work — binaries, protocols, cryptosystems, you name it.

This blog is where I document what I learn, explore what interests me, and occasionally rant about that one challenge that kept me up until 12 AM.

---

## 🧠 What I'm Into

My main interests orbit around the lower layers of computing — the stuff that most people would rather not think about:

- **Reverse Engineering** — Stripping binaries down to their logic. Whether it's a crackme, a packed executable, or a malware sample, I enjoy the process of reconstructing intent from assembly.
- **Cryptography** — From classical ciphers to modern constructions. I find broken crypto implementations in CTFs particularly satisfying to exploit.
- **Pwn / Binary Exploitation** — Stack and heap exploitation, format strings, ROP chains. Still learning, but getting there.
- **Malware Analysis** — Currently diving deeper into this space. Static and dynamic analysis, understanding APT tooling, and figuring out what malware is actually doing under the hood.
- **CTF Competitions** — Where most of the above gets put to the test under time pressure.

Posts here typically cover:
- Malware analysis write-ups and technique breakdowns
- CTF challenge solutions (rev, crypto, pwn)
